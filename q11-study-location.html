Best
Place

Q. What type of location is most effective for your studying?

I focus best at
my desk in my room.

STEP 01

全文を聞こう

まずは止めずに最後まで聞いて、全体の流れをつかもう。

I focus best at my desk in my room.
I can relax and concentrate because I’m by myself.
I usually have some hot tea while I study, and that’s probably when I feel happiest.
Before exams, though, I often go to the library because I can stay focused longer there.
It’s just as comfortable as studying in my room.

STEP 02

チャンクごとに滑らかに読めるように練習しよう

全文音声から切り出した音声を聞いて、同じリズムで読もう。
